# Chassis Notes

Design priorities:

1. Keep the chassis light.
2. Couple the motors mechanically to the body so vibration reaches the legs.
3. Keep battery mass centered until tests show a better bias.
4. Leave room for a physical power switch.
5. Avoid sharp edges and detachable parts.

Suggested first rig:

- Thin plastic plate or small 3D printed sled.
- Two ERM motors mounted left and right.
- Four to eight angled bristle bundles.
- Battery centered with temporary tape or hook-and-loop.
